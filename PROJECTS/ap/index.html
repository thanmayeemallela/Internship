Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh is a dynamic state on India's southeastern coast.
Famed as the "Rice Bowl of India," it features a massive 974 km coastline along the Bay of Bengal.
The capital is Amaravati, andVisakhapatnam serves as the largest city and primary economic hub.Widely known as a top producer of rice, cotton, tobacco, and chillies.
It is also one of India's leading states in horticulture and fruit production.
Dominates India's marine exports, contributing more than 70% of the nation's total output.
Cities like Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, and Tirupati are growing rapidly as IT and industrial centers, specializing in pharmaceuticals and textiles.
Home to major commercial ports like Visakhapatnam Port, Krishnapatnam, and Gangavaram.
Telugu is the classical and official language of the state.
It is the birthplace of the classical dance form Kuchipudi and ancient, globally celebrated textile arts like Kalamkari
Renowned for its bold flavors, aromatic spices, and fiery curries, with staple rice-based delicacies.
The state boasts pristine hill stations like the Araku Valley and scenic bio-diverse ecosystems like the Coringa Wildlife Sanctuary.
