About Andhra Pradesh

Andhra Pradesh is one of the most beautiful states in India. From the beaches of Visakhapatnam to the fertile Godavari lands, the state is filled with culture, spirituality, food, and tourism.

Andhra Pradesh is famous for Telugu traditions, historical temples, delicious spicy cuisine, rivers, festivals, and warm hospitality.

Popular Districts

East Godavari

Famous for Konaseema beauty, coconut trees, Godavari river, and rich Telugu culture.

West Godavari

Known for temples, green fields, aquaculture, and peaceful villages with traditional Andhra lifestyle.

Krishna

Home to Vijayawada, Kanaka Durga temple, Krishna river and tasty food.

Vizag

Popular for beaches, hills, tourism spots, seafood and modern city life.

Guntur

Famous for chilli market, Amaravati history, temples and Andhra cuisine.
