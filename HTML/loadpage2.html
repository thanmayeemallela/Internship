From one highway dhaba to Andhra's largest.

Rayudu Gari Military Hotel started with a simple promise — serve authentic Telugu food cooked the way it has always been cooked. No shortcuts. No compromise. Just the real flavours of Andhra Pradesh.

Today, thousands of families, travellers, and food lovers stop at our doors on NH-16 every single day. The crowd is proof. The flavour is the reason.

From the Mutton Dum Biryani that people drive hours for, to the Gongura Mutton that tastes like home — every dish is a celebration of Telugu culinary heritage.

This is Vindu Bhojanam. This is Rayudu Gari.
